FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What IT services does Bjorn Technologies offer?

We offer five core services: Custom Software Development (web apps, mobile apps, APIs, system integration), IT Consulting (technology strategy, process optimisation, cloud migration, security audits), Website & Media (website design, e-commerce, UI/UX, brand identity), Hosting & Domains (domain registration, managed hosting, business email, DNS and SSL), and Local AI Solutions (LLM fine-tuning, on-premise AI deployment, custom AI assistants, data privacy compliance).

Where are you based, and do you work outside Darwin?

We're based in Darwin, NT (ABN 98 694 561 021) and work with businesses across Darwin, Palmerston and the Top End, including rural and remote sites. Most work can also be delivered remotely, so we take on clients Australia-wide.

What is a local AI solution and why does it matter?

A local AI solution deploys artificial intelligence models directly on your own infrastructure, rather than routing sensitive data through third-party cloud services. Your data stays private, meets Australian data sovereignty requirements, and the AI can be fine-tuned to your specific business processes. We specialise in on-premise LLM deployment and custom AI assistant development.

Do you provide web hosting, domains and business email?

Yes. We register and renew .com.au and other domains, host websites, set up business email, and manage DNS and SSL certificates. If your domain or hosting is scattered across old providers, we can consolidate it and look after renewals so nothing lapses.

How do I get started?

Fill out our contact form or email hello@bjorntechnologies.com.au to book a free consultation call. We'll discuss your project goals, timeline, and specific requirements, then recommend the most effective solution for your business.

Can you build custom software tailored to my specific business processes?

Yes. We build web applications, mobile apps, APIs and integrations around the way your business already works — replacing spreadsheets, double handling and off-the-shelf tools that don't quite fit.
