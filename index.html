We offer four core services: Custom Software Development (web apps, mobile apps, APIs, system integration), IT Consulting (technology strategy, process optimisation, cloud migration, security audits), Website & Media (website design, e-commerce, UI/UX, brand identity), and Local AI Solutions (LLM fine-tuning, on-premise AI deployment, custom AI assistants, data privacy compliance).

Do you work with businesses across Australia?

Yes. Bjorn Technologies is an Australian registered company (ABN: 98 694 561 021) that partners with businesses across Australia. We deliver lean, effective technology solutions tailored to your needs — no bloat, just results.

What is a local AI solution and why does it matter?

A local AI solution deploys artificial intelligence models directly on your own infrastructure, rather than routing sensitive data through third-party cloud services. Your data stays private, meets Australian data sovereignty requirements, and the AI can be fine-tuned to your specific business processes. We specialise in on-premise LLM deployment and custom AI assistant development.
